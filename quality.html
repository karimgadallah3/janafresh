Our Quality Management System

At Jana Fresh, quality management is at the heart of everything we do. Our comprehensive Quality Management System (QMS) is designed to ensure that every product leaving our facilities meets the highest international standards for safety, quality, and consistency.

We implement rigorous quality control procedures at every stage of production, from farm selection and cultivation monitoring to harvesting, sorting, packing, and cold storage. Our quality assurance team conducts regular inspections and laboratory tests to verify compliance with specifications and regulatory requirements.

Our commitment to excellence is demonstrated through our certifications from leading international bodies including BRC (British Retail Consortium), GLOBALG.A.P., ISO 9001, and Sedex. We continuously invest in training our staff, upgrading our facilities, and adopting the latest food safety technologies to maintain our position as a trusted supplier in the global marketplace.

Our Quality Process

1

Farm Selection

Careful selection of farms based on soil quality, water availability, and sustainable practices

2

Cultivation Monitoring

Regular monitoring of crop health, pest management, and compliance with good agricultural practices

3

Harvest Control

Optimal harvest timing, careful handling, and immediate cooling to preserve freshness

4

Sorting & Grading

Automated and manual inspection to ensure only premium quality products proceed to packing

5

Laboratory Testing

Comprehensive testing for pesticide residues, microbiology, and nutritional content

6

Traceability

Complete tracking from farm to customer with detailed documentation at every step
